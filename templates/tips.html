🥩 Beef vs. Mutton: How to Differentiate

New to the kitchen and confused at the butcher shop? Here is how to easily tell them apart:

  • 🔴 Color: Beef meat is a darker, deep cherry-red color. Mutton (goat/sheep) meat has a lighter, pinkish-red hue.
  • ⚪ Fat Color: Beef fat is distinctly yellowish-white. Mutton fat is stark pure-white and much firmer.
  • 🦴 Bone Size: Beef bones are thick, wide, and heavy. Mutton bones are much thinner, cylindrical, and lighter.
  • 👃 Texture & Aroma: Mutton has a strong, distinct gamey smell and a fine-grained, tender texture. Beef has a milder, classic meat aroma with a coarser grain.

🧂 Chef's Secret Ratios & Kitchen Hacks

🧂 Standard Salting Ratio

As a rule of thumb, use 1 teaspoon of salt per 1 kg of meat or vegetables. For rice, use 1.5 teaspoons of salt per 2 cups of dry rice when boiling.

🧅 The Onion Base Hack

For a smooth desi gravy (Salan), fry your onions until golden brown, then blend them with yogurt before adding to the curry. This prevents raw onion pieces floating in your karahi!

❄️ Freezer Meat Storage

Always wash and portion meat into smaller recipe-sized ziploc bags *before* freezing. Thawing and refreezing a large block of meat ruins the texture and breeds bacteria!
