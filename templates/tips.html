Beef vs. Mutton Identification

Confused at the butcher shop? Here is how to easily identify beef and mutton cuts:

  • 🔴
    Meat Color: Beef is a deep, dark cherry-red. Mutton (goat/sheep) has a much lighter pinkish-red hue.
  • ⚪
    Fat Appearance: Beef fat is distinctly yellowish-white. Mutton fat is stark pure-white and much firmer.
  • 🦴
    Bone Thickness: Beef bones are thick, wide, and heavy. Mutton bones are slender, cylindrical, and lighter.
  • 👃
    Aroma & Texture: Mutton has a characteristic gamey aroma with fine tender grain. Beef has a classic mild meat aroma with coarser grain.

Cooking & Boiling Timetables

Standard boiling estimates to achieve melt-in-mouth tenderness:

Ingredient ⚡ Pressure Cooker 🍲 Open Pot
Chicken 🍗 5 - 8 mins 20 - 25 mins
Mutton / Goat 🐑 15 - 20 mins 45 - 60 mins
Beef 🥩 25 - 35 mins 1.5 - 2 hours
Beef Paya (Trotters) 🥣 45 - 60 mins 4 - 6 hours
Chana Daal (Chickpeas) 🌾 10 - 12 mins 35 - 45 mins
💡 Pro Tip: Always let the pressure cooker steam release naturally for 10 minutes (Dum) before opening the valve to preserve juicy meat fibers.
